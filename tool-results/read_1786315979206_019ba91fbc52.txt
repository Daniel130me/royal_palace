    50→    50→# 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION
    51→    51→
    52→    52→Royal Palace Health Care Digital Platform is a managed healthcare ecosystem connecting:
    53→    53→
    54→    54→* Patients.
    55→    55→* Doctors.
    56→    56→* Dentists.
    57→    57→* Pharmacies.
    58→    58→* Laboratories.
    59→    59→* Diagnostic centres.
    60→    60→* Logistics personnel.
    61→    61→* Platform administrators.
    62→    62→
    63→    63→The platform is not simply a doctor-directory website or a telemedicine application.
    64→    64→
    65→    65→Its primary differentiator is **continuity of care**.
    66→    66→
    67→    67→The patient journey connects:
    68→    68→
    69→    69→```text
    70→    70→Healthcare Need
    71→    71→      ↓
    72→    72→Provider Discovery
    73→    73→      ↓
    74→    74→Appointment Booking
    75→    75→      ↓
    76→    76→Payment
    77→    77→      ↓
    78→    78→Consultation
    79→    79→      ↓
    80→    80→Structured Clinical Record
    81→    81→      ↓
    82→    82→Prescription / Laboratory Request / Referral
    83→    83→      ↓
    84→    84→Pharmacy / Laboratory / Receiving Provider
    85→    85→      ↓
    86→    86→Result / Fulfilment / Additional Care
    87→    87→      ↓
    88→    88→Follow-Up
    89→    89→      ↓
    90→    90→Continuous Patient Health Record
    91→    91→```
    92→    92→
    93→    93→Everything implemented in the prototype must support this connected journey.

   123→   123→

   125→   125→* Doctor → Appointments.
   126→   126→* Doctor → Dashboard.
   127→   127→* Admin → Appointments.
   128→   128→
   129→   129→When the doctor completes the consultation:
   130→   130→
   131→   131→```text

   134→   134→POST /prescriptions
   135→   135→POST /laboratoryRequests

   140→   140→This cross-portal behaviour is essential.

   167→   167→
   168→   168→```text
